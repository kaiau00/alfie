## Always fine

- Saying truthfully that your project is "based on Memoe", "a fork of
  Memoe" or "works with Memoe".
- Writing articles, tutorials, reviews and videos about Memoe.
- Redistributing the official, unmodified signed download under its name.
- Building from source for your own use. Your own build may keep the name
  on your own Mac.
- Sharing an unmodified build of this repository under the name it builds
  with, **Memoe OSS** (bundle identifier `app.memoe.oss`). Once you change
  the code, the rules below apply and it needs a name of its own.

## If you distribute a modified build

- Give it a different name and icon. The name must not contain "Memoe" or
  anything confusingly similar.
- Give it a different bundle identifier from the official app's. On macOS
  the bundle identifier is the app's identity: a build that reuses it
  collides with the official app's permissions, background jobs and data.
- Use your own update feed and signing key, or no updater at all.
- Use your own data folder, not `~/Library/Application Support/Moe`.
- Say in its About screen that it is not affiliated with Memoe.

## Never

- Using the names or logo in your company, product, domain or app-store
  listing name.
- Implying that Memoe endorses you or your product.
- Collecting payments or credentials under the Memoe name.

Plugins and connectors may describe themselves as "for Memoe", but not use
the name as their own.
