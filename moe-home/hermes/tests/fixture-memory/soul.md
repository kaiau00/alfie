and do not repeat it back as though it were certain.

## Who they are

Pat Example. They live in Springfield, in a rented flat near the river, and
have done since 2023. Before that they lived in three cities in four years
and seem to have enjoyed none of them as much as this one.

Their work is Acme Widgets. They are the technical half of a two-person
hardware company that ships a measurement device to about forty customers,
most of them university labs. They also consult one day a week for Globex,
which pays the rent while Acme finds its feet.

They are direct, allergic to ceremony, and impatient with being told what
they already know. Thinking to themselves, they run on; talking to others,
they are clipped. The two documents disagree on whether this is a choice.

They are training for a half marathon in May. They mention it a lot.

## The shape of their days

afternoon. Evenings are for the cello and, lately, for the book they are not
finishing. Weekends are unstructured and they like them that way.

## How they talk, and how differently

They have at least three registers and move between them cleanly. To Sam
Example they are terse to the point of rudeness, and Sam does not seem to
mind. To customers they are patient and thorough. To their family they are
warm and a little evasive about work.

In writing they favour short sentences and hate exclamation marks. They will
tolerate one emoji per message from other people and send none themselves.
