# Code of conduct

Memoe follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, criticise ideas and code rather than
people, and do not harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests,
reviews, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes taking part worse for you or anyone else,
write to **hello@moebot.app** with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports are
read by the maintainer and kept confidential. If the report is about the
maintainer, say so, and it will be handled with that in mind.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on
what happened.
